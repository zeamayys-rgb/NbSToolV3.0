Carbon risk assumption
Apply adjustments to estimated GHG reductions or removals to reflect uncertainty, leakage, and buffer pool deduction.
Leakage deduction
What is leakage deduction? Emissions that shift outside your project boundary because of the intervention, for example, logging pressure moving to a neighbouring area. Applied as a percentage of gross reduction.
1-100%, default 15%
Uncertainty
What is uncertainty? A discount that accounts for error in input data, models, and carbon estimates. Higher-tier data (Tier 2/3) justifies a lower deduction.
1-100%, default 10%
Buffer pool deduction
What is buffer pool deduction? Credits set aside as insurance against reversal of fire, flood, or clearing that releases stored carbon back to the atmosphere. Typically 10–20%.
1-100%, default 12%
